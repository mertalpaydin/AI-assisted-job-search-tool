Statistical Protocol
  • Null Hypothesis: $H_0: p_{\text{applied}} = p_{\text{skipped}}$
  • Alternative: $H_1: p_{\text{applied}} \neq p_{\text{skipped}}$ (Two-Tailed)
  • Significance Ceiling: $\alpha = 0.05$
  • Target Power: $1 - \beta = 0.80$
  • Pooled variance used for Z-score; unpooled used for 95% Wald CI.
